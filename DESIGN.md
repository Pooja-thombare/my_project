## Brand & Style

This design system blends contemporary luxury travel commerce with the sensory richness of classic Indian heritage. The visual language balances evocative cultural warmth against structured, high-utility transactional clarity. It is designed for domestic and international voyagers seeking immersive, multi-region Indian circuits—from Himalayan retreats and Rajasthani palaces to Kerala backwaters and Chettinad culinary corridors.

The emotional tone balances reverence with kinetic discovery: dignified, richly textured, and unencumbered by kitsch. The aesthetic merges **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Heritage Layering**:
- Warm, architectural ivory surfaces that echo hand-pressed paper and polished sandstone instead of sterile digital whites.
- Deep jewel tones and earth pigments that lend gravitas to booking flows, itinerary chronologies, and immersive imagery.
- Subtle geometric motifs inspired by stepwells (*baolis*) and *jaali* lattices used purely as hairline borders, watermarks, and rhythm markers.
- Confident high-contrast typography pairing an aristocratic transitional display serif with an ultra-legible, human-centric sans-serif for rigorous pricing, route timing, and booking details.

## Colors

The palette draws its values from traditional Indian pigments: raw terracotta (*geru*), royal indigo dye (*neel*), saffron marigold (*genda phool*), and lush sanctuary flora. 

- **Primary (`#161D6F` - Royal Navy):** Anchors primary brand elements, top-level navigation, primary call-to-actions, and authoritative pricing summaries. Evokes heritage royalty, timeless overnight rail journeys, and oceanic stability.
- **Secondary (`#C84B31` - Terracotta Spice):** Applied to active navigation indicators, key promotional badges, selection states, and itinerary milestone nodes. Brings grounded warmth and architectural resonance.
- **Tertiary (`#E76F51` - Saffron / Marigold Glow):** Used for focal accentuation—deal tags, seasonal circuit callouts, rating stars, alert pips, and interactive hover shifts.
- **Neutral (`#2B2625` - Deep Umber Charcoal):** Replaces pure digital black for all typography, high-contrast borders, and structural icons, retaining tactile warmth against light canvas layers.

### Contextual & Surface Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#F4EFE6` - Raw Parchment):** The foundational global background under layovers and page bodies.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#FBF8F2` - Warm Ivory):** The standard background for itinerary cards, search modules, and dialog sheets.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Bone):** Used exclusively for high-priority interactive inputs, popovers, and elevated modals.
- **Accent Forest (`#0B4619` - Malabar Emerald):** Designated for verified eco-tours, luxury wildlife sanctuaries, confirmed availability statuses, and certified local guide tags.
- **Divider Hairline (`#E8E0D2`):** Low-contrast structural rules separating route stops and timetable breakdowns.

## Typography

The typography couples editorial grandeur with crisp modern legibility:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for destination titles, narrative introductions, regional circuit labels, and luxury stay headers. Its dramatic stroke contrast honors literary expedition logs and architectural inscriptions. It should never be tracked out; keep letter-spacing neutral to slightly snug.
- **System Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Drives all functional UI—booking inputs, dynamic transit timelines, filter bars, pricing tags, and legal disclaimers. Its balanced x-height and open apertures prevent fatigue across data-heavy itinerary builders and comparative pricing matrices.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold with uppercase transformation and deliberate tracking (`0.04em`–`0.06em`) for badge pills, transit mode indicators (e.g., `FLIGHT`, `TRAIN`, `PRIVATE JEEP`), and departure stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable 12-column grid system designed around comfortable, unhurried reading and scanning distances.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns. Screen margin is set to `margin-mobile` (1.25rem / 20px), with `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Cards span 4 full columns with horizontal edge bleeding allowed for destination photo scrollers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns. Margins increase to 2rem (32px), gutters remain 1.5rem (24px). Search bars and itinerary detail panels collapse into tabbed stacked modules.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with a maximum content container width of `1320px`. Canvas margins scale to `margin` (2.5rem / 40px), gutters fixed at `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Accommodates sticky booking summaries, split-screen interactive circuit maps, and modular itinerary feeds.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on intervals of 8px. Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge padding and internal icon-to-label gaps. Use `space-md` (16px) for intra-card content grouping (e.g., price lockup relative to package amenities). Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding and stack margins. Use `space-xl` (40px) to divide days within multi-week itinerary flows.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids cold, artificial gray drop-shadows. Instead, elevation uses **warm terracotta-tinted ambient occlusion** combined with physical sandstone tonal layering.

- **Surface Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F4EFE6`. No elevation. Foundation for page scrollers and split panels.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Default Cards & Sidebars):** `#FBF8F2` with a subtle 1px border (`#E8E0D2`). No blur shadow at rest; rests entirely flat on the canvas to emphasize tactile stability.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Hovered Cards & Booking Bars):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(200, 75, 49, 0.05);`
  - Yields a light, sunlit lift without feeling like detached software geometry.
- **Surface Tier 3 (Dropdowns, Datepickers & Light Modals):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp outer boundary and deep ambient suspension:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -6px rgba(22, 29, 111, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.06);`
- **Surface Tier 4 (Sticky Booking Bar & Floating Route Minimap):** Supported by a warm frosted glass treatment:
  - `background: rgba(251, 248, 242, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-top: 1px solid rgba(232, 224, 210, 0.8);`

## Shapes

The geometric personality of this design system is **Rounded (Level 2)**, delivering balanced corner radii that feel welcoming yet structurally disciplined.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, search fields, itinerary stop pills, route leg tags, and day markers.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Standard curvature for destination overview cards, pricing breakdown pods, image galleries, and booking side-drawers.
- **Hero & Modal Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px):** Reserved for high-impact promotional destination sheets, master booking checkout cards, and map overlay widgets.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Status badges (e.g., `MONSOON SPECIAL`, `ALL INCLUSIVE`), filter tags, counter chips, and standalone circular action buttons (favorite heart, route expander).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Instant Booking / Confirm Route):** Solid Royal Navy (`#161D6F`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius. Height is 48px on mobile, 52px on desktop. Hover state darkens slightly with subtle warm glow. Active press scales down slightly (`0.98`).
- **Secondary Action (Explore Itinerary / Download Permit):** Transparent background with a 1.5px border in Terracotta Spice (`#C84B31`) and text in `#C84B31`. Hover switches fill to Terracotta with pure ivory text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (Day Details / View Notes):** No background or border. Deep Umber text (`#2B2625`) with an interactive underline transition and forward chevron glide.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage / Theme Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally. Rendered in Terracotta tint (Background: `rgba(200, 75, 49, 0.12)`, Text: `#C84B31`).
- **Eco / Sanctuary Badges:** Background `rgba(11, 70, 25, 0.1)`, Text Emerald Forest (`#0B4619`).
- **Interactive Itinerary Filter Chips:** Rounded `0.5rem`, Border: `1px solid #E8E0D2`, Background `#FBF8F2`. Selected state fills with Royal Navy (`#161D6F`), text flips to `#FFFFFF`, and border synchronizes to Navy.

### Tour Cards
- Composed of Surface Tier 1 (`#FBF8F2`) with a top aspect-ratio image container (`16:10`) sporting an internal `rounded-lg` clip. 
- Bottom container contains `Playfair Display` tour title, meta icons for duration, travel mode chips (e.g., `Luxury Train`, `Private Chauffeur`), and price lockup anchored in Royal Navy.
- Hover triggers Tier 2 elevation lift and slight image scale (`scale-105`) with smooth bezier timing.

### Itinerary Chronology Timeline
- Vertical timeline element driven by a 2px continuous dashed line in Terracotta tint.
- Milestone nodes are circular: 32px diameter, `#FBF8F2` fill, 2px solid `#C84B31` ring with centered day numbering in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold.
- Day cards attached to nodes display departure time, transfer vehicle icon, destination landmark, and curated activities in `#2B2625`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Search bar sits on an elevated ivory pod with inputs partitioned by subtle vertical hairpins (`#E8E0D2`).
- Inputs feature `Plus Jakarta Sans` with floating labels. In focus, input displays a distinct 2px outline in Terracotta Spice (`#C84B31`) without default browser ring glow.
- Date and guest picker panels emerge as Tier 3 elevated cards with clear calendrical grids highlighting holiday circuits.

### Route Maps & Status Modules
- Maps utilize custom warm muted cartography (sandstone landmasses, indigo waterways).
- Polyline paths transition from Terracotta Spice (`#C84B31`) to Saffron (`#E76F51`) with pulsating waypoint rings for active live tracking of long-distance rail and road convoys.
